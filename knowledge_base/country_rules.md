# Country-Specific Calling Rules

## General Rules (All Countries)
- Identify the caller as Fordaq at the start of every call
- Offer opt-out at any time
- Do not call numbers on any do-not-call list
- Do not call before 9:00 AM or after 6:00 PM local time
- Maximum 3 attempts per lead before marking as unresponsive

## European Union (GDPR + ePrivacy Directive)
- Personal data must be used only for the purpose it was collected
- Members must be able to opt out of direct marketing at any time
- Record opt-outs and never call again
- If recording the call, disclose at the start: "This call may be recorded
  for quality purposes"
- Automated calls without prior consent are restricted in some EU countries
- Country-specific rules below override general EU rules

### France
- Bloctel opt-out list: do not call registered numbers
- Automated calling systems require prior consent for B2C; B2B rules apply
- Language: French preferred

### Germany
- Cold calling B2B is permitted only if there is a "presumed interest"
- No automated dialling systems without consent
- Language: German preferred

### Italy
- Registro delle Opposizioni: check before calling
- Language: Italian preferred

### Spain
- Robinson list: check opt-outs
- Language: Spanish preferred

### Belgium
- Do Not Call registry: check opt-outs
- Languages: French or Dutch depending on region

### Netherlands
- Infofilter opt-out: check before calling
- Language: Dutch or English

## Saudi Arabia & Gulf States
- Business calls acceptable 9am–6pm local time (avoid Friday)
- Arabic preferred; English widely understood in business
- Friday is a rest day — do not call
- Cultural context: be formal and respectful; decision-making may involve
  multiple parties

## Pakistan & India
- English widely used in B2B timber trade
- Call during business hours (9am–6pm IST/PKT)
- Relationship-first approach: ask how they are before business

## Turkey
- Turkish preferred; English often understood
- Business hours 9am–6pm TRT

## Russia & CIS
- Russian preferred; English sometimes limited
- Calling may be restricted depending on sanctions — verify account status
- Business hours 9am–6pm MSK

## China
- Mandarin preferred; English limited outside major cities
- Business hours 9am–6pm CST
- WeChat often preferred over phone for follow-up
