# Fordaq Product Categories

## Sawn Timber (Softwood)
- Pine (Pinus sylvestris, Radiata pine, Maritime pine, etc.)
- Spruce (Picea abies)
- Fir (Abies)
- Douglas Fir
- Larch
- Cedar
- Dimensions: thickness × width × length in mm (e.g. 50×100×4000)
- Grades: C16, C24; A, B, C, D; visual grades
- Moisture: KD (kiln dried) / AD (air dried) / Green
- Certification: FSC, PEFC, SVLK

## Sawn Timber (Hardwood)
- Oak (European, American White/Red)
- Beech
- Ash
- Poplar / Aspen
- Birch
- Walnut
- Cherry
- Maple
- Tulipwood / American tulipwood

## Tropical Timber
- Teak
- Meranti / Lauan
- Sapele
- Iroko
- Okoumé
- Eucalyptus
- Certification: FSC tropical, FLEGT/VPA

## Logs
- Softwood logs: pine, spruce, fir
- Hardwood logs: oak, beech, birch
- Tropical logs (trade regulated, CITES for some species)
- Measured in m³ or MFbm

## Panels
- Plywood (structural, decorative, marine)
- MDF (medium density fibreboard)
- HDF (high density fibreboard)
- OSB (oriented strand board)
- Particle board / chipboard
- Hardboard

## Pallets & Packaging
- Standard EUR pallets (1200×800 mm)
- One-way pallets
- Block pallets
- Pallet collars / boxes
- Wood packaging materials (ISPM 15 treatment required for export)

## Engineered Wood & Components
- Glulam (glued laminated timber)
- CLT (cross-laminated timber)
- LVL (laminated veneer lumber)
- Finger-jointed boards
- Furniture components, blanks, parts
- Mouldings, profiled timber

## Veneer
- Sliced veneer (face veneer)
- Rotary cut veneer
- Technical veneer

## Flooring
- Solid wood flooring (parquet)
- Engineered flooring
- Laminate flooring
- Decking (terrace boards)

## Cellulose & Biomass
- Wood pulp
- Paper and board
- Wood pellets
- Wood chips
- Firewood / briquettes

## Trade Units
- m³ (cubic metres) — most common for Europe
- MFbm (thousand feet board measure) — USA/Canada
- MT (metric tonnes) — pulp, chips, pellets
- Container: 20ft (~23 m³ loose) / 40ft (~46 m³ loose)
- HQ container (high cube 40ft): more volume
- Lorry load: ~25–30 m³ in Europe
