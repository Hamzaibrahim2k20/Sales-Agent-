# Fordaq Membership Rules

## Free Membership
- Register and create a company profile
- Post limited buyer requests or seller offers
- View company profiles and listings
- Access basic market news
- Limited direct contact with other members (contact limits apply)
- Cannot access full contact details or send unlimited messages

## Paid Membership (Buyer)
- Unlimited access to supplier profiles and contact details
- Direct contact with sellers
- Priority placement of buyer requests
- Market intelligence and price reports
- Active Matching support (depends on package)

## Paid Membership (Seller)
- Prominent listing in search results and directories
- Highlighted offers visible to buyers worldwide
- Direct contact from buyers
- Active Matching (depends on package)
- Microsite option (depends on package)
- Market intelligence

## Membership Tiers
Bronze < Silver < Gold (exact pricing: NEVER quote to members — escalate to sales)

## Expired Members
- Previously paid, now lapsed
- May still have a profile visible but no premium benefits
- Target for renewal

## Renewal Rules
- Renewal pricing and terms: ALWAYS escalate to Fordaq sales team
- Never promise a discount or extended trial without sales approval

## DO NOT COMMIT
- Guaranteed leads or guaranteed buyers/sellers
- Specific pricing or discounts
- Refunds or partial credits
- Trial extensions
- Membership upgrades without sales approval
