# Fordaq — Platform Overview

## What Fordaq Is
Fordaq is the world's leading B2B online platform for the timber and wood-products industry.
It is a marketplace, a company directory, and an industry information hub.

Fordaq connects:
- Buyers (importers, traders, manufacturers, distributors, retailers)
- Sellers (sawmills, exporters, traders, agents, producers)
- Service providers (logistics, insurance, machinery, certification bodies)

## What Fordaq Is NOT
Fordaq is NOT a broker, agent, reseller, distributor or transaction guarantor.
Fordaq does NOT verify the legitimacy or financial standing of its members beyond basic registration.
Fordaq does NOT handle payments, guarantees, letters of credit or logistics.

## Core Products

### 1. Timber Marketplace
Members post buying requests and selling offers.
Buyers find suppliers. Sellers find buyers.
Categories: sawn timber, logs, panels, plywood, MDF, OSB, HDF, pallets,
veneer, flooring, furniture components, paper and pulp, wood energy, machinery.

### 2. Company Directory
Every Fordaq member has a company profile visible to other members.
Profiles include products, countries, certifications, contact details.

### 3. Market Information
News, price indices, market reports, industry statistics.
Available to all members; premium content for paid members.

### 4. Active Matching
Premium feature. Fordaq actively matches buyer requests with suitable sellers
and vice versa. Requires paid membership.

### 5. Microsite
Premium feature. A dedicated company page within Fordaq with
custom content, photos, videos and branding.

### 6. Advertising
Banner advertising, newsletter sponsorship, highlighted listings.
For companies targeting specific buyer/seller segments on Fordaq.

## Geographic Coverage
Fordaq is headquartered in Europe (Belgium).
Members in 100+ countries. Strongest coverage in Europe, with growing
presence in Middle East, Asia, Africa and Americas.

## Languages
Platform available in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese,
Romanian, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Arabic and more.
