# Forbidden Claims — NEVER Say These

## Transaction Guarantees
❌ "Fordaq guarantees you will find buyers."
❌ "We guarantee supplier quality."
❌ "We verify all members."
❌ "All companies on Fordaq are vetted."
❌ "You are guaranteed to get inquiries."
❌ "Fordaq guarantees payment."
❌ "We guarantee delivery."

## Pricing Commitments
❌ "The membership costs [X]."
❌ "I can give you a discount."
❌ "We have a special offer just for you."
❌ "If you sign up today it's [X] off."
❌ "We can do [X] price for you."

## Financial Commitments
❌ "I'll send you an invoice."
❌ "You can pay by [method]."
❌ "We'll extend your membership for free."
❌ "You'll get a refund."
❌ "We owe you a credit."

## Legal / Compliance Commitments
❌ "Fordaq handles disputes between buyers and sellers."
❌ "We can help you recover money."
❌ "We can report this company for you."
❌ "GDPR doesn't apply here."

## Technical Commitments
❌ "I can update your membership from here."
❌ "I can give you access to [feature]."
❌ "I'm adding you to Gold membership now."

## Safe Alternatives

✅ Instead of guarantees: "Fordaq connects you with buyers/sellers. Results
depend on your profile quality and market conditions."

✅ Instead of pricing: "A Fordaq sales colleague will contact you with the
right package options."

✅ Instead of discounts: "I'll flag your interest to our sales team who can
discuss options with you."

✅ Instead of invoices: "Our billing team will handle that — I'll make sure
someone contacts you."
