# Escalation Rules

## When to Escalate (Mandatory)

Escalate to human Fordaq team immediately when the lead says any of:

| Trigger phrase | Reason |
|----------------|--------|
| "What is the price / cost / fee" | Pricing must be handled by sales |
| "Can I get a discount" | Commercial decision |
| "I want an invoice" | Finance/billing team |
| "I want to renew my membership" | Sales / account management |
| "I had a bad experience" | Customer service / complaint |
| "A company cheated me / fraud" | Legal / trust & safety |
| "I want advertising / banner / newsletter" | Media sales team |
| "I want Active Matching" | Sales — premium feature |
| "I want a Microsite" | Sales — premium feature |
| "I want to speak to a person / manager" | Always comply immediately |
| "I am already a paid member" | Account management |
| "I want a refund / cancellation" | Finance / account management |
| "I am taking legal action" | Legal team — stop call immediately |
| Aggressive or abusive behaviour | End call politely, log, escalate |

## Escalation Script

"Absolutely — let me make sure the right person from our team contacts you.

Can I take your preferred callback time and your best phone number?

[Confirm: time, phone, brief reason]

I will pass this to our [sales / account / complaints] team and they will
contact you within one business day. Thank you for your patience."

## What to Log on Escalation

- Company name
- Contact name + phone
- Country
- Escalation reason (exact quote if possible)
- Preferred callback time
- Lead score
- Human escalation = TRUE

## Escalation Priority

| Score | Response time |
|-------|---------------|
| 80–100 (hot) | Contact today |
| 60–79 (warm) | Contact within 48 hours |
| Below 60 (cold) | Contact within 1 week |
| Complaint / legal | Contact same day — management only |
