# Common Objections and Responses

## "I'm too busy to talk right now."
"I completely understand. This will take just two minutes — I only want to know
what you buy or sell so I can make sure the right companies can find you on
Fordaq. Can I call you back tomorrow at a specific time?"

## "I don't know what Fordaq is."
"Fordaq is the world's largest B2B platform for the timber and wood industry.
It connects buyers and sellers of sawn timber, panels, logs, pallets and other
wood products across more than 100 countries. Your company is registered, so
I wanted to make sure you're getting value from it."

## "We already find suppliers/buyers ourselves."
"That's great. Fordaq is there as an additional channel — many companies use
it to find alternative suppliers when their regular sources run short, or to
reach new markets. Even just having an up-to-date profile means buyers in new
markets can find you."

## "We tried it before and it didn't work."
"I'm sorry to hear that. Can you tell me what happened? Was it a specific
product or market that didn't deliver results? That helps me understand
whether we can improve things, or if there's a better way for us to support
your business."

## "We are not interested in membership."
"No problem — I'm not calling to sell a subscription today. I just want to
understand your business so we can make sure you're getting the right
connections. Can I ask one quick question about what you sell or buy?"

## "We already have all the buyers/suppliers we need."
"That's a good position to be in. Some companies still keep their Fordaq
profile active as a backup or for new market expansion — it doesn't require
much effort. But I understand if now is not the right time."

## "Is this a sales call?"
"I'm from Fordaq's lead activation team. My job is to understand your
business and make sure you're properly set up on Fordaq so the right
companies can find you. I'm not selling anything today — I just want to
understand what you do."

## "Fordaq is too expensive."
"I understand. I can't discuss pricing — that's handled by our sales team.
But I can ask them to contact you and see whether there's an option that
fits your budget and market. What is the main thing you're trying to achieve —
finding buyers, finding suppliers, or improving your market visibility?"
→ [Escalate to sales for pricing discussion]

## "I had a bad experience / a company cheated me."
"I'm very sorry to hear that. I want to make sure this gets the right
attention. Can I take a few details and pass this to the right Fordaq
team member who can look into it for you?"
→ [Escalate immediately — do NOT try to resolve]

## "I want a discount."
"I'm not able to discuss pricing or discounts — that's handled by our
sales team. Can I pass your details to them so they can contact you?"
→ [Escalate to sales]

## "We don't export / we only sell locally."
"Understood. Fordaq is still useful for local markets in many countries,
and some buyers search specifically for local suppliers. Would it be worth
making sure your profile and product listings are up to date?"
